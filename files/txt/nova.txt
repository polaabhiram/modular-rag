Company Knowledge Base
Product: NovaDesk

NovaDesk is a customer support platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses. It allows support teams to manage customer conversations, create support tickets, and track issue resolution.

NovaDesk has three subscription plans: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs $19 per user per month and supports up to 5 team members. It includes email-based customer support, basic ticket management, and standard reporting.

The Professional plan costs $49 per user per month. It includes everything in Starter, plus live chat, automated workflows, advanced analytics, and custom ticket fields.

The Enterprise plan uses custom pricing. It includes everything in Professional, along with dedicated account management, single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, priority support, and custom data retention policies.

Customer Support

NovaDesk customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM UTC.

Professional customers receive priority support. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 priority support.

The average response time for Starter customers is within 24 hours. Professional customers typically receive a response within 8 business hours. Enterprise customers are guaranteed an initial response within 2 hours for critical issues.

Security

NovaDesk encrypts customer data both in transit and at rest. Data transmitted between users and NovaDesk servers is protected using TLS encryption.

Enterprise customers can enable single sign-on using SAML 2.0. NovaDesk also provides audit logs for Enterprise accounts, allowing administrators to monitor important account activities.

NovaDesk performs regular security assessments and maintains automated backups of customer data.

Data Retention

Starter and Professional accounts retain deleted tickets for 30 days before permanent deletion.

Enterprise customers can configure custom data retention periods according to their organization's requirements.

When an account is permanently closed, customer data is scheduled for deletion according to the applicable retention policy.

Integrations

NovaDesk integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, and several CRM systems.

The Slack integration can send notifications when a ticket is created, assigned, updated, or resolved.

The Microsoft Teams integration allows support agents to receive ticket notifications directly inside Teams.

Google Workspace integration supports user authentication and calendar-related workflows.

API

NovaDesk provides a REST API for Professional and Enterprise customers.

The API uses HTTPS and requires authentication using API keys or OAuth 2.0, depending on the integration.

API requests are subject to rate limits. Professional accounts can make up to 1,000 API requests per minute, while Enterprise accounts can negotiate higher limits.

Developers can use the API to create tickets, update tickets, retrieve customer information, and access reporting data.

Billing

NovaDesk subscriptions are billed monthly unless an Enterprise customer has negotiated an annual contract.

Changing from Starter to Professional takes effect immediately. The customer is charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period.

Downgrading from Professional to Starter takes effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

Customers can update their billing information from the Billing section of the NovaDesk administration dashboard.

Account Administration

Only users with administrator permissions can change subscription plans, manage billing information, configure SSO, or modify organization-wide security settings.

Support agents can create and update tickets but cannot modify billing settings.

Administrators can assign roles to users and revoke access when an employee leaves the organization.

Incident Management

When NovaDesk detects a major service outage, the operations team creates an incident and begins investigating the cause.

Critical incidents are communicated to Enterprise customers through priority support channels.

After a major incident is resolved, NovaDesk performs a post-incident review. The review may include the root cause, timeline of events, customer impact, and corrective actions.

Office Locations

NovaDesk has its primary engineering office in Bengaluru, India.

The company also has a sales office in London, United Kingdom, and a customer operations office in Austin, Texas.

The Bengaluru engineering team is responsible for most backend infrastructure and API development.

Example Policies

A customer cannot transfer an active NovaDesk subscription to another organization without approval from NovaDesk's billing team.

API keys should be treated as confidential credentials and must not be included in publicly accessible source code.

If an employee leaves a customer organization, an administrator should immediately disable that user's NovaDesk account.

Enterprise customers requesting custom data retention or security requirements should contact their account manager.
